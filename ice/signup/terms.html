Daily Feed Terms and Conditions

The serious-ish rules for receiving spiritually useful notifications.

  1. You agree to provide an email address that belongs to you, your guardian, or at least someone who knows you.
  2. You may receive scripture, study questions, reading plans, and reminders to stop scrolling and read something meaningful.
  3. We cannot guarantee that every message will arrive at the exact moment you need it. We can only guarantee that technology will choose an interesting moment.
  4. You promise not to blame the Daily Feed if a verse makes you think, improves your day, or causes you to clean your room.
  5. You may unsubscribe whenever you want. No dramatic farewell letter is required, although a tiny violin is optional.
  6. This is a class project, so these terms are for fun and are not intended to replace actual legal advice.
